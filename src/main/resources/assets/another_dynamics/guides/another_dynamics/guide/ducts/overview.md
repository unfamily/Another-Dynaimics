# Overview

All **ducts currently available** in your game (kinds that did not load simply do not appear):

<DuctGrid />

Place them like pipes. Matching kinds connect together and to inventories, tanks, or energy handlers on their faces. Right-click a face to open the **node** GUI: extract/insert, filters, modules, and display options.

**Project Duct** is a helper block for building or converting temporary pipe layouts into real ducts.

See also: [Transport kinds](transport_kinds.md), [Nodes and GUI](nodes_and_gui.md), [Filters](filters.md), [Modules](modules.md).
