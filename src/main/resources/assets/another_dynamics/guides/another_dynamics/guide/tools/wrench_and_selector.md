## Bulky Wrench

Wrench for ducts and compatible blocks: disconnect faces, rotate, or remove nodes without breaking the whole pipe where the mod allows it.

## Remote Node Selector

Right-click a block to **bind** a remote destination for extractor / retrieve modes. The tooltip shows the bound position. Select another block to rebind.
